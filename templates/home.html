{# ---------- protection ---------- #}

// built to protect the people doing the work

cap

8-hour days

A company's working day can't be longer than 8 hours, and attendance only counts inside it. Tasks can't be given outside working hours or on public holidays.

the payroll guard

Every company's funds are checked against what its people have earned, in one currency at the day's rate. If it falls short, the owners are warned; 24 hours on, the company is halted and every employee is told.

paid {{ cycle_every }}

Salary counts from the day you join: at least your position's minimum. Every payment carries the bank's reference and a screenshot, with a payslip and a receipt in your statements.

fair in, fair out

Joining and leaving need approval, and you only leave once every salary due to you is paid. A company's terms are shown before you join, and any change is yours to accept.

chats, live

Message anyone in your companies, one to one or in groups, from the chat button on every page. Company chats are labelled (its owner, managers and HR can read them); personal chats are for the two of you. Encrypted at rest.

meetings with captions

Video calls with screen sharing and recording. Whisper captions everyone in English as they talk, and the local AI keeps notes. Only the people invited can join, one call per company at a time.

tasks & a calendar

Tasks with deadlines on the company's clock, on a calendar that shows your region's public holidays. Likes and dislikes come with a reason, and nobody can pile them on.

your language

Pick a language in the header: Hindi, Arabic (right to left), German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian or Chinese. Pages are translated on this server, and your choice follows your account.

salaries in any currency

Pay a salary in INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD or AED. It's checked against the minimum and the company's funds at the day's rate, and the payslip keeps the rate it used.

Spam and floods are paused automatically and the AI warns the admin about repeat offenders. The admin can ban accounts and IP addresses with a reason, sees every IP during an overload, and can switch on single company mode.

{# ---------- hiring ---------- #}

// hire and get hired

python job · pays in USD · no weekends

an AI company finder

Say what you want in your own words. It reads pay in any currency, hours, weekends, freelancing and the city, finds the companies that fit and tells you what you left out.

matched by meaning

Your profile and CV are matched with companies by what they mean, not by keywords. Owners find people the same way, and a job offer always comes with its salary.

verified on both sides

Every listed company's registration and location are checked, and everyone in a company has an identity proof on file. Bank details are checked too, across borders.
